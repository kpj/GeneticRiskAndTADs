TAD Statistics
==============

Summary statistics and distributions of called Topologically Associating Domains (TADs),
including:
* Domain count distributions across chromosomes, datasets, and caller algorithms.
* Domain length and inter-TAD gap distributions.
* Genomic coverage and chromosomal domain densities.
* Total genomic footprints across defined boundary geometries.
* Pairwise concordance and overlap similarity (Jaccard index) across caller configurations.
